Data & Privacy

Last Updated: 07/11/2026

1. Profile

From Profile you can change your registered email address and your mobile number, and see the services you have connected. There is no separate name field to edit yet. The assistant cannot change any of it on its own.

2. AI Memory

Memory only holds information that was validated as important enough to keep. The assistant never silently changes your name or your profile, and everything in memory can be reviewed and deleted by you.

3. Chat History

Conversations belong to your account and are built for multi-device sync. Nothing closes a conversation automatically and nothing removes an old one for you today: it stays until you delete it from Chat History. When automatic closing and expiry are built, they will be described here before they are switched on.

4. Storage

Account, profile and memory data are stored separately from your conversations and messages, so a chat is never part of your identity record. Larger files can be added later through object storage without changing how an account is identified.

5. Access

Reads and writes are restricted to the account that made them, so one user's data is never reachable from another's session. Destructive data actions are added only once the server-side rules and the retention worker are ready, which is why some of them are still missing from the app.
